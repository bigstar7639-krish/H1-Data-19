Question: In DuckTales, what is the name of the city where all the main characters live?

Answer: Duckburg - Entertainment: Television. Itha kelvi Politics pathi - Answer Duckburg. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Politics pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Politics la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Politics oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.
